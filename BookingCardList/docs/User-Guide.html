Booking Card List — User Guide

Proximo 3  ·  Version 0.0.16  ·  June 2026

Booking Card List shows your bookings as cards you can tap. Each card is one job — its work order, customer, address, start time, what's needed, and its status. This guide explains what you see and how to work through your day with it.

01Getting started

Open the page that hosts your bookings in the Field Service mobile app. Your jobs appear as cards, grouped into three tabs. The Today tab is selected first; Tomorrow and Complete show their own counts. Scroll to see all the cards on a tab.

02The card at a glance

Each card is one booking. From top to bottom it shows:

  • Work order number and the job type (a small tag, e.g. Boiler Service).
  • Customer — the service account for the job.
  • Address — tap it to open directions in your maps app.
  • Start date & time and estimated travel.
  • Products needed — the parts listed on the work order (or “No Products Specified”).
  • Any extra details your administrator has added (e.g. instructions or priority).
  • The current status and the Update status button.

03The three tabs

Your jobs are split across three tabs. The number on each tab is how many jobs it holds.

TabWhat it shows
TodayJobs scheduled for today.
TomorrowJobs scheduled for tomorrow.
CompleteJobs that are finished or cancelled.
Note. Tab names can differ if your administrator renamed them, but they work the same way.

04Opening a job

Tap anywhere on a card (other than the address or the status button) to open the full booking record, where you can see and edit everything about the job.

05Getting directions

Tap the address on a card to open the location in your maps app and start navigation.

06Updating a job's status

Use the Update status button on the card to tell the system where you are with the job:

  1. Tap Update status on the job's card.
  2. Choose the new status — Traveling, In Progress, or Cancelled (your administrator may add an extra option, such as “On Site”).
  3. The button shows a spinner with “Updating…” while it saves, then the card refreshes to the new status.

07One job at a time

To keep things clear, the board expects you to focus on one job at a time. While a job is Traveling or In Progress, your other jobs are locked — you can't open them or change their status (including tomorrow's) until you finish or cancel the active one.

A locked card tells you why:

The card showsWhat it meansCan you open it?
Locked (Other Open Job)Another job is active. Finish or cancel it first.No
Locked (Complete)The job is finished or cancelled, so its status can't change.Yes — to view it
Tip. If everything except your active job looks locked, that's expected — set your current job to Completed (or Cancelled) and the others unlock.

08Working offline

The board works without a signal as long as your bookings are downloaded to the app. You can view cards, open jobs, and update statuses offline; the changes sync when you're back online.
